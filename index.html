Built for Australian sole traders

Tools for your logbook and cents-per-km records. You stay in control of accuracy.

Cents-per-km deduction calculator

Estimate your claim under the ATO cents-per-kilometre method — 88c/km for 2024–2025 and 2025–2026, 91c/km for 2026–2027. Updated each year when the ATO publishes the new rate, capped at 5,000 business km.

12-week ATO logbook

Tracks your continuous 12-week logbook period with real-time progress, valid for 5 years once established.

Simple trip tracking

Start and end each trip in one tap. Distance is calculated on your device, then snapped to real roads for accuracy. We store your route and trip summary — never a live location, and never when a trip isn't running.

Reports for your accountant

Generate a financial-year summary of business kilometres and your estimated deduction.

Business purpose prompts

Prompts you to record a specific work reason on business trips. You decide what to enter; you remain responsible for whether it is adequate for your claim.
