Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 July 2026

Tallyka ("we", "us") provides a vehicle logbook app that helps Australian sole traders, contractors and employees record work trips for their own tax record-keeping. Tallyka is not the ATO, is not endorsed by the ATO, and does not guarantee that any record or claim will be accepted. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Who we are

Tallyka is currently operated as an individual project based in Australia, pre-commercial registration. Contact: tallyka.info@gmail.com.

We collect location only while you are actively recording a trip. That may continue briefly if the screen locks or the app is in the background during an active trip, so distance is not cut short. There is no always-on tracking when no trip is active. Distance is calculated on your device; GPS accuracy can vary and you remain responsible for checking that trip records are correct before you rely on them for tax purposes.

How we use it

  • Provide the logbook, distance calculation and ATO report features.
  • Generate financial-year reports and, when available, email a report to an address you specify.
  • Manage your subscription, once billing is live.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

We use trusted processors only to run the service, including our authentication provider (Clerk), our mapping and route-snapping provider (Mapbox), our hosting/database provider, and, once configured, a payment processor and an email-sending provider. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cross-border data transfer

Providers including Clerk, Mapbox and our hosting/database provider are based in, or process data in, the United States. Disclosing personal information to these overseas recipients means the protections of Australian Privacy Principle 8.1 may not apply to that transfer, and Australian law may not be the only law that governs how your information is handled once it reaches them. By using Tallyka you acknowledge and consent to this cross-border disclosure, in line with APP 8.2(b). We only work with providers who apply their own security and privacy commitments to the data they process for us.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, vehicles, trips, logbook periods and authentication identity from our systems, and clears any unsynced data held on your device. Receipt photos stored on your device are removed when you delete the app or your account.

Your rights

Under the APPs you can request access to, or correction of, your personal information, and you can withdraw consent to non-essential processing at any time by contacting us. You can also export a complete copy of the personal information we hold about you at any time, in-app: Settings → Your data → Export my data. This download includes your profile, vehicles, trips and logbook periods as a single file. For anything the export doesn't cover, contact tallyka.info@gmail.com. You can also raise a concern with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Data breach notification

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected users and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), without undue delay once the breach is confirmed.

Children

Tallyka is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in-app.
